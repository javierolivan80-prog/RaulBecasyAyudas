# Dirección de fotografía — las 6 imágenes del despacho

Prompts detallados para generar (o encargar) las seis fotografías de la web.
Los prompts van **en inglés**: los generadores de imagen responden bastante
mejor y los términos técnicos de cámara y película están mucho mejor
representados en su entrenamiento.

---

## Bloque de estilo base

Pega esto **al final de los seis prompts**. Es lo que hace que las seis
parezcan del mismo reportaje y no seis imágenes sueltas.

```
Shot on Sony A7R V, natural window light only, no flash. Kodak Portra 400
colour response: warm neutrals, desaturated navy-grey shadows, skin and wood
slightly warm, greens muted. Fine 35mm film grain, gentle halation on the
brightest highlights, mild lens vignetting. Off-white walls, never pure white.
Realistic imperfections: fine dust on surfaces, faint fingerprints on glass,
paper edges slightly curled, objects not perfectly aligned. No HDR, no
oversaturation, no clarity slider, no glossy CGI sheen. Editorial documentary
photography, quiet and unstaged, photographed at eye level.
```

## 1 · La sala de consulta, vacía

**Qué cuenta:** la silla vacía es la foto. Dice «aquí te vas a sentar tú».
**Formato:** 16:9 horizontal → cabecera de «Cómo se trabaja»

```
Interior photograph of a small, calm law office meeting room in Tel Aviv,
empty and waiting for a client. A light oak table with a visible open grain
and one faint water ring near the edge. Three chairs in pale grey wool
upholstery; the near one is pulled out and turned slightly, not aligned with
the others. On the client's side of the table: a closed A5 notebook with a
soft linen cover, a black ballpoint pen resting diagonally across it, and a
plain glass of water two-thirds full with condensation beading down the side
and a small ring of moisture on the wood. A tall window on the left with
half-drawn unbleached linen curtains; bright Mediterranean late-morning light
rakes low across the tabletop, throwing long soft-edged shadows to the right
and revealing dust motes suspended in the shaft of light. In the far corner, a
monstera in a matte terracotta pot, one lower leaf yellowing at the tip.
Plastered off-white walls with faint roller texture and a small scuff mark
near the skirting board. Warm oak floor with visible grain and one shallow
scratch catching the light. 28mm lens at f/4, ISO 200, 1/125s, camera at
seated eye level, composed from the doorway with slight one-point perspective,
deep focus front to back.
```

## 2 · Él trabajando, visto desde la puerta

**Qué cuenta:** alguien concentrado en tu asunto. No posa, no mira a cámara.
**Formato:** 4:5 vertical → sección «Perfil»

```
Candid documentary photograph of a lawyer working alone at his desk, seen from
the office doorway. The dark wooden door frame occupies the left quarter of
the frame, heavily out of focus in the foreground, framing the scene. The man
is in three-quarter profile, turned toward a laptop, one hand resting on an
open case file, the other holding a pen mid-thought. He is not looking at the
camera and is unaware of it. He wears a white shirt with the sleeves rolled to
the forearm, slightly creased at the elbow from wear. Realistic skin texture
with visible pores and fine stubble, natural asymmetry, no retouching or
smoothing. On the desk: a stack of manila folders squared off unevenly, a
half-drunk cup of black coffee, a phone face down, a tangle of charging cable
running off the edge. Soft directional light from a window camera-right rims
his cheek and shoulder and falls off into cool shadow on the left side of his
face. Bookshelves behind him fall into soft focus. 35mm lens at f/1.8, ISO
400, 1/160s, focus locked on his eye, shallow depth of field with the door
frame and the background dissolving.
```

## 3 · El escritorio en detalle

**Qué cuenta:** el oficio. Papel, tinta, uso real.
**Formato:** 3:2 horizontal → separador entre secciones

```
Overhead flat-lay photograph of a working lawyer's desk, shot straight down
from above. An open case file with densely typed pages, the text deliberately
soft and illegible at this focal plane; the top sheet has a folded corner and
two short passages underlined by hand in blue ink. A black fountain pen lies
across the page at an angle, its cap beside it, not parallel. A pair of
tortoiseshell reading glasses rests half-open on the upper right, one lens
catching a soft window reflection. A cardboard folder tied with a worn elastic
band, the band slightly stretched and shiny with use. A ceramic cup with a
faint coffee tide-line inside. The surface is aged oak with visible pores, one
old ink dot, and a scattering of fine dust catching the raking light. Light
enters low from the upper left, sculpting the edges of the paper and casting
soft graduated shadows to the lower right, giving the paper fibres visible
texture. 50mm lens at f/5.6, ISO 200, 1/100s, tripod directly overhead,
everything in sharp focus except the printed text.
```

## 4 · Las manos explicando

**Qué cuenta:** la promesa de la web hecha imagen — «tu caso, explicado».
**Formato:** 1:1 cuadrada → junto a la cita, o en «Primera consulta»

> ⚠️ Es la foto más difícil de generar: las manos siguen siendo el punto débil
> de los generadores. Genera muchas variaciones y revisa dedos y nudillos uno
> por uno, o sácala con una cámara, que es la más fácil de las seis de montar.

```
Close-up documentary photograph of two pairs of hands over a document on a
wooden table, faces out of frame entirely. The lawyer's hand enters from the
right holding a black pen, its tip resting on a specific paragraph, the index
finger extended and the wrist relaxed. Opposite, the client's hands rest on
the table edge, fingers loosely interlaced, one thumb over the other. Both
hands anatomically correct with exactly five fingers each: visible knuckle
creases, tendons under the skin, short clean fingernails, fine hair on the
forearm, natural skin texture with pores and faint veins. The document is a
printed form with soft, illegible text and an official-looking stamp in the
upper corner, its ink slightly uneven. Warm side light from a window at
camera-left models the knuckles and casts soft shadows across the paper.
Shallow focus on the pen tip and the paragraph beneath it; the client's hands
fall gently soft. 85mm lens at f/2.8, ISO 400, 1/200s, camera low and close
at table height.
```

## 5 · La estantería, sin el cliché

**Qué cuenta:** autoridad, pero de un despacho que se usa.
**Formato:** vertical o 3:2 → fondo de «Perfil» o de las credenciales

```
Photograph of one section of a working lawyer's bookshelf, not the whole wall.
Legal codes and statute volumes in deep navy, oxblood and faded green cloth
bindings, of visibly different heights and thicknesses, leaning slightly
against one another with a gap where one volume has been pulled out. Spines
worn at the head and tail, gold lettering partly rubbed away, one book with a
paper slip protruding as a bookmark. Beside them, three ring binders labelled
in handwritten marker on masking tape, stacked flat rather than upright. A
small trailing pothos in a plain ceramic pot on the shelf below, one tendril
hanging down in front of the spines. A framed photograph face-on but small and
angled away from the light, its image indistinct. Fine dust visible along the
top edge of the shelf. Soft indirect daylight from the left, falling off into
warm shadow on the right of the frame. 50mm lens at f/2.0, ISO 400, focus on
the middle row of spines, the shelves above and below softening into bokeh.
```

## 6 · Los dos países sobre la mesa

**Qué cuenta:** la especialidad, sin una sola palabra.
**Formato:** horizontal → cabecera de «Áreas de práctica»

```
Overhead still-life photograph of international legal paperwork spread across
a dark walnut desk. A burgundy passport lying closed and slightly angled, its
embossed cover catching the light, the crest soft and non-specific. Beside it,
two official certificates on textured cream paper, partially overlapping: one
with a circular embossed dry seal that catches raking light in relief, the
other with a rectangular ink stamp in faded violet, its edges imperfect where
the stamp lifted unevenly. All printed text is deliberately out of focus and
unreadable, with no legible names, numbers or dates anywhere in the frame. A
paper clip holds two sheets together at the corner, slightly bent. A pair of
reading glasses sits at the top edge of the frame, partly cropped. Low
directional light from the upper right rakes across the paper, revealing its
fibre texture, the raised relief of the dry seal and the subtle curl of the
certificate edges. 50mm lens at f/5.6, ISO 200, tripod overhead, sharp on the
seals and the paper texture.
```

## Notas de ejecución

**Las dos con persona, mejor con cámara.** Las fotos 2 y 4 son las más fáciles
de montar en la vida real (él en su mesa; unas manos sobre un papel) y las más
difíciles de generar de forma convincente. Además, si la 2 tiene que ser
*él*, un generador no reproduce su cara de forma fiable, y para una web
profesional una foto real pesa mucho más.

**Revisa el texto de las imágenes.** Los generadores inventan letras que
parecen idiomas pero no lo son. Aquí juega a favor —no queremos documentos
legibles—, pero comprueba que no aparezca nada que parezca un nombre, un
número de expediente o hebreo mal formado.

**Consistencia.** Genera las seis con la misma semilla de estilo y el mismo
bloque base. Si una sale con una luz distinta, repítela: seis imágenes que no
casan se notan más que una imagen mediocre.

**Resolución.** Mínimo 1600 px de lado; para la 1 y la 6, que van a ancho
completo, mejor 2400 px.

**Confidencialidad.** Ningún documento legible, ningún nombre, ningún número
de expediente, ninguna cara de cliente. Para un colegiado esto no es estética.
